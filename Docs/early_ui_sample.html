Full Strategic Narrative

The "Q3 Decarbonization" campaign launched successfully on Monday with a multi-channel approach spanning email marketing (Mailchimp) and paid social (LinkedIn). Initial pipeline generation is healthy, but efficiency is currently compromised due to a bottleneck in the mid-funnel web experience.

Key Observations

  • Strong Top-of-Funnel: Email open rates exceeded the baseline by 14%, indicating the "Net-Zero Blueprint" subject line is highly resonant with the audience.
  • The Wednesday Bottleneck: When LinkedIn ads launched on Tuesday, traffic spiked by 400% on Wednesday. However, Python analysis of the joined GA4 dataset reveals an 82% bounce rate exclusively for traffic tagged with utm_source=linkedin.
  • CPA Inflation: Because we spent $2,000 on LinkedIn to drive traffic that immediately bounced, our blended Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) has temporarily spiked to $215.00.
